# Evacuation Protocol

## About the Game

**Evacuation Protocol** is a realistic PvPvE survival FPS set in a massive city devastated by war, a deadly virus, and rogue AI systems.

Players must explore the city, fight both hostile AI and other players, solve puzzles, rescue survivors, gather resources, and ultimately escape before it's too late.

The game focuses on tactical combat, exploration, teamwork, and survival rather than simply eliminating every opponent.

---

## Story

A large-scale war and a biological disaster have pushed the city into total collapse.

Military AI units, security systems, and automated defense networks have gone out of control after being infected by an unknown virus. The city has become a restricted evacuation zone.

As one of the remaining survivors, your mission is to stay alive, help civilians, uncover the truth behind the disaster, and find a way to escape.

---

## Gameplay

* First-Person Shooter (FPS)
* PvPvE (Players vs Players vs Environment)
* Up to 20 players per match
* Smart AI enemies and powerful bosses
* Puzzle-solving mechanics
* Escape-based objectives
* Dynamic weather system
* Day and night cycle
* Character customization
* Weapon customization
* Level progression and experience system
* Rescue civilians for additional rewards
* Home Base progression between matches
